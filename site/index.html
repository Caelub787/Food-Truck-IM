Questions

Do my drivers need to download anything?+

No. It runs in the phone's web browser. They can add it to their home screen so it opens like any other app.

What if there's no signal at the event?+

Counts, loads, and waste save on the phone and send by themselves, in order, when signal comes back.

Do I have to enter sales?+

No, sales are optional. Drivers can tap in what they sold, or you can upload a sales file from your register. With sales, the app compares what was used with what was sold.

Can other businesses see my numbers?+

No. Each business only ever sees its own trucks, stock, sales, and people.

Does it work on a computer too?+

Yes. Owners and managers get a full computer view with wide tables, reports, and spreadsheet downloads.

Is it only for one kind of truck?+

No. Coffee, shaved ice, tacos, or anything you sell from stock. You name your own supplies, units, products, and lists.
