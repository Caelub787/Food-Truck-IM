Client bookings

Let clients book your truck online.

Schools, parties, weddings, and offices ask for a day and time on your own booking page. You decide.

  • Your own page to share on your website, social media, or in emails.
  • Clients pick a day and time. Days you're full are crossed out.
  • Accept or decline in a tap. The client gets an email either way.
  • Accepting puts it on the schedule with the truck, driver, crew, manager, on site contact, and what to bring. Everyone on it gets the details.

Scheduling

Everyone knows where to be.

  • Plan the week by truck, with a Lead and crew for each shift. Repeat it weekly.
  • Get a warning when a truck or a person is booked twice.
  • Drivers see their own shifts, get reminders, and start the shift right from the plan.
  • Booked events show the address (it opens in maps), who to call on site, and what to bring.

Budget

A budget that fills itself in.

Plan what you'll spend and earn each month, and see how you're really doing.

  • Inventory spending comes from your deliveries. Sales and booked events come in as income.
  • Type in the rest, like fuel, payroll, and repairs, in categories you name.
  • Link your bank to see balances, or type them in. View only: no money ever moves through the app.
  • See the whole year as one sheet, and download it for your accountant.

Reports

Reports that send themselves.

  • A morning email, plus daily, weekly, and monthly reports at the hours you pick.
  • Sales minus costs, waste by reason, free items, and how each driver is doing.
  • Every report downloads as a spreadsheet.

Questions

Do my drivers need to download anything?+

No. It runs in the phone's web browser. They can add it to their home screen so it opens like any other app.

Does any money go through the app?+

No. Linking a bank only lets you see balances for your budget. Nothing can be paid or moved. Booking requests don't take payment either.

Can my clients book us online?+

Yes. Turn on your booking page, set the days and hours you take bookings, and share the link. Nothing is booked until you accept it.

What if there's no signal at the event?+

Counts, loads, and waste save on the phone and send by themselves, in order, when signal comes back.

Do I have to use every part?+

No. Inventory works on its own. Scheduling, bookings, sales, and count checks are switches you turn on when you want them.

Can other businesses see my numbers?+

No. Each business only ever sees its own trucks, stock, sales, money, and people.

Is it only for one kind of truck?+

No. Coffee, shaved ice, tacos, or anything you sell from stock. You name your own supplies, units, products, and lists.
